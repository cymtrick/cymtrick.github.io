About

I'm Prashanth Varma Dommaraju, a security engineer in Amsterdam. I work on penetration testing and vulnerability research, and I have a background in building distributed systems and ML infrastructure.

I recently finished an MSc in Computer Science at the University of Amsterdam and Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam, where my thesis was on structure-aware fuzzing of Kubernetes. This blog grew out of the Secure and Trustworthy AI course in that programme: the posts are my notes on how models are attacked, what the formal guarantees actually say, and what the numbers in the papers look like side by side.

Every claim in a post is cited to a primary source, every chart is drawn from reported numbers, and corrections are welcome. The source for the site, including the data behind each figure, is on GitHub.

Find me at github.com/cymtrick or prashanthdomma@gmail.com.
